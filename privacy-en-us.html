Privacy policy

How Gasel handles data, location and external services.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Controller and contact

Ismael Ares is the controller of Gasel. For privacy queries or support: contact@gasel.uk.

Data and purpose

The app creates no accounts and requests no name, email, phone number, payment data or advertising identifiers. Approximate or precise location is optional and is used while the app is open to show nearby gas stations, order results, center the map and calculate distances. You can use the app without granting it and search an area manually.

External services

The app downloads public fuel prices from the Spanish Government. To calculate road distances, it sends the coordinates of your location or of the searched area, and those of nearby stations, to the public OSRM server (router.project-osrm.org), a third-party service, without personal identifiers. Place search and the area name use Android geocoding, which may send the searched text or the coordinates to the system provider (usually Google). The map uses Google Maps SDK, and Google may process request and device metadata under its privacy policy.

Security and sharing

Communications use HTTPS. We do not sell data or include advertising or our own analytics. If the app closes unexpectedly, it saves a technical report on the device that is never sent automatically: it only leaves the device if you choose to send, share or copy it. Sharing a station or opening a navigation app is an action you start.

Retention and changes

Settings, discount plans, custom names and icons, and downloaded data remain on the device until you delete them. If Android backup is turned on, the system may include your settings in it; the saved area, the data cache and the error report are excluded. This policy will be updated when the app’s data practices change.
