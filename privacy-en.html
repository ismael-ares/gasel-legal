Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Controller and data

Ismael Ares is the controller of Gasel. The app creates no accounts and requests no name, email, telephone number, payment data or advertising identifiers.

Location is optional and is only used while the app is open to show nearby petrol stations, order results, centre the map and calculate distances. You can search an area manually.

Services, security and retention

The app uses public price and discount sources, Android geocoding, OSRM when needed and Google Maps SDK. Communications use HTTPS; we do not sell data or include adverts or our own analytics.

Settings, favourites, discount plans and downloaded data remain on the device until deleted. Enquiries: ismael.ares@pm.me.
