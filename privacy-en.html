Privacy policy

How Gasel handles data, location and external services.

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Controller and contact

Ismael Ares is the controller of Gasel. For privacy queries or support: contact@gasel.uk.

Data and purpose

The app creates no accounts and requests no name, email, telephone number, payment data or advertising identifiers. Approximate or precise location is optional and is used while the app is open to show nearby petrol stations, order results, centre the map and calculate distances. You can use the app without granting it and search an area manually.

External services

The app downloads public price and discount data, may use Android geocoding and OSRM when needed, and uses Google Maps SDK. Google may process request and device metadata under its privacy policy.

Security and sharing

Communications use HTTPS. We do not sell data or include ads, our own analytics or crash reporting. Sharing a petrol station or opening a navigation app is an action initiated by you.

Retention and changes

Settings, favourites, discount plans and downloaded data remain on the device until you delete them. This policy will be updated when the app’s data practices change.
