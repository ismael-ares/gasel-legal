PAU GASOiL privacy policy

Controller: Ismael Ares. Contact: ismael.ares@pm.me.

PAU GASOiL has no accounts, ads or its own analytics. Optional approximate or precise location is used while the app is open to show nearby petrol stations, order results, centre the map and calculate distances. You can use manual search without granting location.

Settings, favourites, discount plans and downloaded data remain on the device. Map-camera coordinates and distance snapshots are kept outside Android backup and device transfer. Clear app storage or uninstall the app to delete local data.

The app uses public fuel-price and discount sources, Android geocoding, OSRM routing when needed, and Google Maps SDK. Google Maps may process request/device metadata, IP address, a pseudonymous SDK identifier, crash metrics and map interactions under Google's privacy policy. Connections use HTTPS. Sharing and navigation are initiated by you.

External providers have their own retention policies. We do not sell data. This policy changes when the app's data practices change.
